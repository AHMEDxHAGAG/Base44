## Layout and behavior

White surface. Wide: capped 90 px gutters, bottom padding capped at 160 px, two columns with the heading `Frequently asked questions` fixed to the left and a roughly 705 px accordion at the right. Mobile stacks heading above the accordion with 20 px gutters. Items use 2 px dark top separators and generous 28–32 px button padding. Questions are Dazzed medium/display; answers are Geist 400, `clamp(16px,.9375vw,18px)`, line-height 1.44, max 832 px.

First item is open initially; only one item opens at a time. Buttons have plus/minus visuals and correct `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, and labelled `role=region`. Closed regions are `aria-hidden`, inert, and use grid rows `0fr`; open uses `1fr`. Transition grid rows 400 ms cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1); answer opacity 300 ms ease-out. Disable motion when reduced motion is requested.
